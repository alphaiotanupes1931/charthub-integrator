# Engineering rules

- Coach trade reviews use encrypted owner/account-bound five-minute tickets, an atomic one-time proposal claim and separate explicit confirmation; recheck account, risk, grade, demo lock and live quote before execution. Why: model output and chat text must never authorize or replay broker orders.
- TradeLocker account type comes from account details, independently of API environment; account state, positions and protective orders use broker-configured columns. Why: balances do not identify account type, and fixed position indexes corrupt P&L and stop visibility.
- Coach broker snapshots use only user-linked credentials, never platform market-feed credentials. Why: an unconnected trader must not see the platform owner's brokerage account.

- Classic liquidity-draw detection and proximal entries live in `src/lib/detector/liquidity-{pools,draw}.ts`; the named rollout is separate from v2 candidates. Why: live and replay must share one definition without silently changing frozen candidate models.
- Liquidity locks persist by canonical symbol and exact OANDA host/mid feed using versioned compare-and-swap; all constituent timeframes must use that feed. Why: concurrent rescans cannot resurrect stale draws or mix price scales.

- Chart data refreshes redraw overlays without fitting the viewport; fit only the first nonempty instrument/timeframe dataset or a newly applied scan pattern. Why: preserve the trader's zoom and pan during live updates.
- Chart-dependent effects key on the chart instance and ignore callbacks/cleanup for removed instances. Why: boolean readiness can stay true across recreation and retain disposed overlay subscriptions.
- Demo scheduling gates new entries only; trade management and run logging continue on skipped schedule ticks. Why: existing positions still need protection after entry hours or an hourly fill.
- Autopilot evaluations persist in scan_log before proposal execution and remain separate from broker journal profit; use fill-required replay and the recorded exact feed when available. Why: missed or blocked setups must be reviewable without inventing realised gains.
- Dashboard and AI Signals accuracy use an authenticated aggregate-only platform RPC: scan target/stop outcomes separate from closed executed journal trades carrying reported P&L. Why: cross-user rates must expose no private rows and must not call replay guesses broker-verified profits.
- Invalidated liquidity draws reset only on opposing closed structural breaks toward unswept pools and require a newly formed protected entry zone. Why: avoid indefinite lockout without chasing a wick or overriding pool direction with Daily bias.

- Live entry prices come only from the model named in each signal's `entry_model`; switching an instrument requires an entry in `ENTRY_MODEL_V2_LIVE` (src/lib/detector/entry-detector.ts). Why: a silent entry-model swap went unnoticed for a week.
- Any change to entry logic must keep `tests/unit/entryGoldenSet.test.ts` green or regenerate `tests/golden/entry-windows.json` deliberately in the same change. Why: frozen windows catch accidental entry-price drift.
- Rules carry an evidence tier in `src/lib/analysis-models/evidence-tiers.ts`; only tier A may block, B may adjust grade/size, C/D record only, and tiers move only with a logged `rule_evidence` row. Why: stops untested assumptions becoming gating logic.
- Entry candidates are compared at equal risk and the same target, with unfilled orders scored 0R. Why: tighter inner levels otherwise inflate quoted R:R.
- The backtest rig (research/rig) must import setups only from `src/lib/detector/`; never reimplement detection there. Why: a research copy that drifts from live is the main cause of backtest/live disagreement.
- Rig runs must register their pre-registration before scoring and may open a holdout once per pre-registration (`research/rig/registry.ts`). Why: the comparison count and single-use holdout are inputs to the statistics.
- Trader-type questions, scoring weights, coach/strategy/risk mappings and first-week tasks live only in `src/lib/trader-profile/config.ts`; screens and server code read from it. Why: Marcus tunes the rules without touching UI or server code.
- Quiz answers carry into signup via a server-stored short code (`quiz_drafts`) plus a localStorage copy, never URL-only. Why: Google sign-in and the separate marketing domain drop query strings and storage.
- Journal trades carry an immutable `lockedPlan` (src/lib/journal-lock.shared.ts) set on first save; the verify tick scores it separately from edited levels. Why: shows whether moving stops/targets helped or hurt.
- Coach trader memory is built deterministically from scans, journal and chats in `src/lib/trader-memory.shared.ts`, not by an extra AI call. Why: no per-message cost and it stays testable.
- Retention email timing and selection rules (morning picks, scanner wins) live only in `src/lib/retention-emails.shared.ts`; the cron route just reads them. Why: keeps send rules testable and in one place.
- New signups land on `/first-scan` after onboarding, before the dashboard. Why: every user sees and tracks one live setup in their first session.
- Before publishing a directional scan, the planner checks past scan_log results for the same symbol/timeframe/direction via `src/lib/scan-track-record.shared.ts`; it moves the grade at most one letter, never blocks, and only past the minimum sample. Why: lets the scanner learn from its logged outcomes inside evidence-tier B limits.
- Autopilot `demo_only` refuses any order whose resolved account env is not practice/demo (`demoOnlyRefusal` in src/lib/autopilot-live.server.ts); unknown env counts as live. Why: test runs must never reach a real-money account.
- Classic chart patterns (double top/bottom, head and shoulders, flags, triangles) are detected only in `src/lib/detector/chart-patterns.ts` under evidence tier B (`context.chart_pattern`): a confirmed pattern moves the grade one step, forming ones only add a note. Why: the rig and live scanner must share one detector, and patterns stay non-blocking until logged results justify more.
- Premium/discount (buy below / sell above the 50% line of the dealing range) is read only in `src/lib/detector/dealing-range.ts`, at the evidence tier set in `evidence-tiers.ts` (`context.premium_discount`). Why: backtest `premium-discount-v1` found no edge yet, so it stays a note until logged results justify moving the grade.
- Order flow and volume (delta, CVD, POC, buy %) do not feed direction, confidence or grade caps while `ORDER_FLOW_IN_GRADES` in `src/lib/agents/planner.server.ts` is false; they print as information only. Why: the trader grades on Daily bias, location and entry, not flow.
- Liquidity-draw late-entry caps (late-leg continuation past `LATE_LEG_LIMIT`, opposite pool inside the stop) and the 1H-CHoCH draw cancel live only in `src/lib/detector/liquidity-draw.ts`; coach grade mismatches are corrected via `src/lib/coach-grade-guard.ts`. Why: the US30 Nov 5 A long lost 1R after the 1H had turned, and the coach then contradicted the card.
- Daily/4H bias caps grade and size (at worst C) but never returns NO ENTRY or picks direction; Demo C/D trades risk at most `LOW_GRADE_MAX_RISK_PCT`, and BTC/ETH stay off auto trading in `evaluateRails` (forex auto trades; GBP/USD stays under review). Why: the liquidity draw decides direction, and low grades are traded only to collect settled results by grade.
- Journal rows placed by TradeMind take broker-reported exit and P&L from `src/lib/journal-broker-sync.shared.ts` (matched by symbol, side, size), dated on the New York close day; the verify tick runs it. Why: guessed P&L on the entry day left close days blank.
- Autopilot has no account-wide open-position cap; it limits stacking per symbol (`MAX_POSITIONS_PER_SYMBOL` in autopilot.shared.ts). Why: one stacked pair locked out every other instrument for a day.
